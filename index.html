Security & data handling

Underwriting software touches sensitive financial documents. We describe posture plainly so compliance and ownership can sign off.

  • Core (desktop): Files stay on hardware you control. No mandatory cloud corpus of your merchants’ statements for the analyzer to function.
  • CRM tier (optional): When you enable server-backed collaboration, scope networking, backups, access controls, and calendar-backed callbacks as part of enterprise onboarding — not as a footnote.
  • Vendor hygiene: Prefer offline OCR where configured; outbound links for public-record checks open in the user’s browser — no silent third-party data brokerage from this product surface.

Details are confirmed in your order form / BAA path before production deployment.

Frequently asked

Straight answers — update contact details on this page before you go live publicly.

Do you replace my funders’ portals?

No. Statement Shield helps you prepare and defend a file. Submissions still follow each funder’s process.

Is this only for MCA?

The scoring language, scrub grammar, and position logic are MCA-native. Other products may be added later, but the soul of the product is cash-advance underwriting.

Cloud or desktop?

Core is built as a desktop workstation on Windows and macOS. CRM unlocks optional server-backed team workflows when you’re ready — same brand, staged rollout, with a callback calendar on the top package and mobile apps for notifications.

Mac, iPhone, Android?

Core is planned as feature parity on Windows and macOS (Mac distribution follows your packaging timeline — we’re not leaving Mac shops behind). Core + CRM includes a shared callback calendar; native iOS and Android apps are on the roadmap so reps get push nudges when a merchant is due for a return call.

What do I change before launch?

Replace statementshield.example in meta tags, robots.txt, and sitemap.xml with your domain; set the contact email in the form handler inside js/main.js.
